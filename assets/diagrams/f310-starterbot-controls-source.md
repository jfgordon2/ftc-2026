# Logitech F310 default StarterBot controller map

Prepared September 30, 2026. Applies to gamepad 1 in the standard 6WD vendor
TeleOp and the team `StarterBotTeleOp` baseline. The aim-assist draft has a
separate A/RB behavior documented in the lesson.

Photos: Logitech, [F310 product page](https://www.logitechg.com/en-us/shop/p/f310-gamepad).

- [Front view](https://resource.logitechg.com/c_fill,q_auto,f_auto,dpr_1.0/d_transparent.gif/content/dam/gaming/en/products/f310/f310-gallery-1.png)
- [Shoulder view](https://resource.logitechg.com/c_fill,q_auto,f_auto,dpr_1.0/d_transparent.gif/content/dam/gaming/en/products/f310/f310-gallery-5.png)

Annotation method: image-generation edit using both official product views.
Arrow positions checked against the source images. In the shoulder view, the
controller's right shoulder is on the viewer's left; RB is above RT.

Mapping checked against the saved goBILDA vendor TeleOp and team baseline:
`arcadeDrive`, trigger subtraction, and `launch`. The lesson text key includes
the feeding-related intake boost and release behavior.
