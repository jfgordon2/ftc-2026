Observed
The student watched the complete skill, pointed to the important controls or result, and can say what the person doing it was checking.
Supported
The student performed the skill while another person gave step-by-step prompts or corrected an action.
Independent
The student performed the skill safely, checked the visible result, and explained it with only a reminder of the goal.
Can Coach
The student guided another person with questions, noticed safety and result checks, and let that person keep control of the work.
